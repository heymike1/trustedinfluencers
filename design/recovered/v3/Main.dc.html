How a profile gets verified
01
Anyone adds a creator. A name, a platform and a handle. The profile is public immediately and shows public data only.
02
The creator claims it. No email checks, no screenshots — they sign in to the exact social account through the platform’s official OAuth.
03
The account ID must match. Same channel or account ID as the profile, or the claim fails.
04
Analytics are imported, not typed. Content, retention, audience and engagement come from the API in the background and are re-synced daily.
